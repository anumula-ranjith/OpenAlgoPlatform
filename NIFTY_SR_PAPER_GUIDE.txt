Rules fixed before the first run on 25 November 2026.

Contract: NIFTY01DEC2625350CE, NFO, one lot of 65 units, long calls only.
This is a distinct rolling-range strategy. It uses no opening-range or EMA
entry signal, no prior-day reconstructed prices and no option shorts or puts.

RULES FIXED BEFORE EVALUATING RETURNS

1. Support = lowest NIFTY index low of the PRIOR 20 completed five-minute
   candles within the current regular session. Resistance = their highest high.
   Exclude the signal candle from those 20 bars. No cross-session window.
2. Buy when a new completed five-minute NIFTY candle CLOSES strictly above
   that resistance. A touch or intrabar high above resistance is insufficient.
3. Execute at the option candle OPEN after that signal candle completes.
   Entries are allowed from 11:00 through before 14:00 IST. At most one entry
   per day. Freeze the entry's support and resistance levels for that trade.
4. Exit when a completed NIFTY candle closes strictly below that frozen support,
   the option premium touches a 15% stop or 30% target, or at 15:05 IST.
   Premium thresholds use the modeled entry fill including costs of slippage.
5. A downside breakout while flat causes no call entry. This fixed-contract
   version does not model buying puts. It does not automatically roll contracts.

DATA AND EXECUTION BOUNDARIES

Use the already-downloaded actual NIFTY and call OHLCV, with manifest/hash checks.
The intended previous-day-high/low variant was not tested because saved intraday
index data lacks the 15:15 and 15:20 candles, and a read-only request to the
current broker did not return daily index history. No missing highs/lows are
invented. The rolling levels above depend only on complete earlier intraday bars.

Require full regular index coverage through 15:00 and full positive-volume option
coverage through 15:05 BEFORE evaluating a day's signals or outcomes. Missing
sessions remain unscored. No forward fill or replacement entry is permitted.

Default adverse slippage is 0.10% on each side, with adverse 0.05 tick rounding.
Use the existing Analyzer's pure fee engine with 20 INR brokerage per executed
order plus statutory charges, schedule dated 23-Nov-2026, rounded per fill.
At candle open, exits have priority: time, premium stop, premium target, support
failure. Gaps fill at the open. Intrabar threshold touches use the level before
slippage. Stop wins if the same candle touches both stop and target.

The source files contain offline signal/policy and simulation functions only.
They do not submit orders, change accounts, register strategies or schedule runs.
Raw market history and generated reports remain local in ignored tmp/.
